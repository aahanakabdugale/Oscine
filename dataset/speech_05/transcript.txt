They may require dozens of high-disco engineers and it may cost millions or tens of millions of dollars to build an AI system. And the large tech companies, particularly the ones with hundreds of millions or even billions of users, have been better than anyone else at making these investments pay off because, for them, a one-size-fits-all AI system, such as one that improves web search or that recommends that the products for online shopping can be applied to this very large number of users to generate a massive amount of revenue. But this recipe for AI does not work once you go outside to tech and internet sectors to other places where, for the most part, there are hardly any projects to apply to a hundred million people or that generates comparable economics.
